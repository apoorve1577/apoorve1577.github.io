Research
Recovery from Compromised AI Models
Model Lineage · Blast Radius · AI Supply Chain Security
Ongoing research on the recovery half of AI supply chain security. Existing tooling verifies a model at the moment you adopt it, but nothing answers what happens when a foundation model is discovered compromised months later, after hundreds of models have already been fine-tuned from it. Building a typed lineage graph with blast radius queries and an automated recovery planner. A working prototype shows that a small gap in lineage recording causes models to be falsely reported as unrecoverable, pushing operators toward expensive retraining when a cheap rollback was available.
